The Story of AuraRing

AuraRing was founded in 2024 by a team of biomedical engineers, software architects, and luxury designers. Our mission was simple: to shrink bulky, intrusive medical telemetry into a beautiful titanium band that fits seamlessly into your life.

Unlike massive smartwatches that require daily charging and disrupt sleep comfort, AuraRing fits on your finger, where the blood flow is closer to the skin surface, ensuring 3x more accurate cardiovascular readings.
